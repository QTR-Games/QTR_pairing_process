# KLIK KLAK — Tip Sheet

One page to keep next to you at the table. Full detail is in the
[User's Guide](klikklak-users-guide.md).

---

## The 30‑second loop

1. Name the **opponent**, fill both **rosters**, set who **goes first**.
2. **Tap cells** to rate matchups (red = dodge, amber = even, green = wanted).
3. Read the **verdict**: is the round **Live**, **Already won**, or **Cannot be
   won**?
4. At the table, open the **Round** tab and follow the prompt one pick at a time.

There's **no Save button** — everything saves as you type.

---

## Read the numbers

| Number | Means | Use when |
|--------|-------|----------|
| **Guaranteed** | Pessimistic floor (assume a mirror, perfect play) | You must **not lose** |
| **Typical** | Realistic middle (they play their own board) | You must **win** |
| **Ceiling** | Best still reachable with perfect play | You're chasing points |

Two currencies: **Round‑win %** (chance to take 3+ games) and **Rating points**
(your raw totals). Close boards can total the same but land on opposite sides of
a coin flip — trust the **%**. Flip it in **Settings → Show numbers as**, or
per‑card.

---

## Verdict chips at a glance

- 🟡 **Live** — still in play.
- 🟢 **Already won** — every pairing wins; extra is bonus.
- 🔴 **Cannot be won** — play for bankable points, not the win.
- **Too close to call** — genuine coin flip.
- **Not rated yet** — move a cell off dead‑even to wake it up.

---

## Dodge, protect, trade‑off

- **Dodge** your worst matchup? The app says **Forced** (can't), **Free**
  (refuse it for nothing), or **Costly** (−X% to refuse).
- **Protect**: *exposed* = their bad matchup repeats and can be forced; *shielded*
  = it's a lone cell they can refuse.
- **Trade‑off (safest vs. boldest)**: only appears when the safe opener and the
  bold opener differ — safe protects your floor, bold chases your ceiling.

## Live‑round prompts

| Prompt | Do this |
|--------|---------|
| **Put a player up** | Nominate one of yours |
| **Which player did they put up?** | Record their opener |
| **Offer two against [name]** | Offer a pair of yours |
| **Which two did they offer…?** | Record their pair, pick one |
| **… — forced** | Last pair, no choice |
| **Round complete** | All five seated |

Committed pairings lock under **Tables set**. **Restart** clears the round.
Set how chatty it is in **Advice during a round** (Full / Just the picks / None).

## Gestures

- **Tap a cell** → rate it.
- **Long‑press a cell** (phone) / **hover** (desktop) → opening cost + dodge price.
- **Long‑press an opponent's name** (Longshanks boards) → their faction/lists.
- **Menu** (header) → back to home.

---

## Back up (do this before every new build)

**Menu → Back up and restore**

- **Save a copy** / **Copy** / **Show** to export.
- **Restore** = merge; **Replace all** = wipe then import (destructive).
- Boards live **on this device only** — a new install can clear them.

---

## Install one‑liners

- **iPhone/iPad**: Safari → **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android**: event laptop runs `npm run phone:install` (no public link).
- **Desktop**: run the `KLIK.KLAK…setup.exe`; on the SmartScreen warning click
  **More info → Run anyway**. It **auto‑updates** itself after that.
